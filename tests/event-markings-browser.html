Event markings and interval harmonies

Standard markings sit opposite the actual stem, or above when no stem is printed; legacy placement preferences cannot override that rule. Road interval figures keep their musical side: 5 above F can add C above; b3 below Bb can add G below. Harmony tones and ornament auxiliaries never become the next road’s reference, and markings do not change written time.
